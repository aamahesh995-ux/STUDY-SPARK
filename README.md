# 📚 Study Spark — Smart Study Planner

## 📖 Project Overview

Study Spark is a smart study-planning web application designed to help students organize their academic activities, manage time effectively, track progress, and improve productivity. It combines study planning with interactive brain-refreshing activities to make learning more engaging and enjoyable.

## ❗ Problem Statement

Students often struggle with time management, multiple subjects, assignment deadlines, lack of motivation, and tracking their learning progress. Traditional to-do lists may not provide all the tools students need to plan, monitor, and maintain productive study habits in one place.

## 💡 Our Solution

Study Spark provides a centralized platform where students can organize study tasks, manage deadlines, monitor progress, and take productive breaks through quizzes, puzzles, and memory games.

## ✨ Key Features

- 📅 **Study Planning:** Organize subjects, tasks, and deadlines.
- ✅ **Task Management:** Add, update, and track study tasks.
- 📊 **Progress Tracking:** Visualize completed and pending activities.
- ⏱️ **Focus Timer:** Support focused study sessions using a Pomodoro-style timer.
- 🧠 **Brain Refresh Zone:** Explore quizzes, puzzles, riddles, and memory games.
- 💬 **Motivational Quotes:** Encourage consistent learning habits.
- 🏆 **Gamification:** Brain XP and achievements to motivate students.
- 🎨 **User-Friendly Interface:** A colorful, responsive, and student-friendly design.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js
- **Backend:** Node.js and Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **Database Integration:** Mongoose
- **Authentication:** JWT and bcrypt
- **Data Visualization:** Recharts
- **API Communication:** REST APIs

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Students organize their subjects and study tasks.
2. They set deadlines and manage their available study time.
3. They update tasks as they complete them.
4. The dashboard helps them monitor their progress.
5. Students use the Brain Refresh Zone for interactive learning breaks.

## 🎯 Target Users

- School students
- College and university students
- Competitive examination aspirants
- Students who want to improve time management and study habits

## 🌍 Impact and Benefits

Study Spark aims to reduce missed deadlines, improve organization, encourage consistent study habits, and make learning more enjoyable. By combining planning, progress monitoring, and interactive activities, it helps students manage their academic responsibilities in one platform.
